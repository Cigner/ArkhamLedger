# Who uses this

Three roles, and one of them barely exists.

## Investigator

A player. Their entire obligation is to say when they are free, and the
application is built around making that take thirty seconds on a phone in a
queue.

They see: their campaigns, the sessions they were invited to, the calendar where
they answer, aggregate availability, and the confirmed date.

They never see: who else said what, anybody's priority including their own
exact one, or the ranking screen.

What they get told: that a session is looking for a date, one reminder if they
have not answered, and the date once it is confirmed — with a calendar
attachment.

## Keeper of Arcane Lore

Runs the game, and the person this application was built to stop being a
scheduling clerk.

They do everything an Investigator does — a Keeper is also a player and answers
like one — plus: create sessions, choose who is invited and how much each one
matters, set the deadline and the quorum, read named availability, run the
search, and confirm a date.

Their availability is a **hard constraint**, never a preference. There is no
session without them.

A campaign can have several Keepers, and all of them are hard constraints:
a co-Keeper who cannot make it is not a spare.

## Owner

Not a role, an attribute: whoever created the campaign, transferable. A Keeper
with three extra powers — archive the campaign, transfer ownership, remove or
re-role members — plus the Discord webhook, because a webhook posts to a room
the whole group reads.

An owner cannot leave their own campaign without transferring it first.

## Administrator

One person, a few times a year. Creates accounts, hands over activation links,
disables accounts, and reads the operations screen.

**An administrator is not privileged inside campaigns they are not a member
of.** The operations screen reports counts and names no campaign.
