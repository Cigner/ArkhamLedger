# Arkham Ledger logo

## Construction

The mark combines three product concepts:

- an open ledger for campaign records;
- a twelve-part clock ring for session scheduling;
- a restrained organic form for cosmic horror.

The source uses only SVG geometry, flat fills, and strokes. It has no raster textures, gradients, filters, or external images. The horizontal lockup uses Cinzel, matching the application display typeface. Convert the wordmark to outlines before exporting artwork for environments where Cinzel is unavailable.

## Usage

- Use `arkham-ledger-mark.svg` for the favicon, collapsed sidebar, app icon, and square placements.
- Use `arkham-ledger-lockup.svg` on sign-in screens and wide brand placements.
- Preserve clear space equal to at least one clock-tick length around the mark.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, distortion, or recolour individual elements.
- At sizes below 32 px, omit the inner clock circle and page-detail strokes if manual simplification is required.
